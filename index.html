ABOUT

jaag is a DJ, event curator, writer, and photographer raised and currently living in the Bay Area.

Her sets dance between genres rather than staying inside one. Breaks, electro, leftfield bass, trance, acid, global percussion, and unexpected vocal moments are woven into her sonic fabric.

She is also the founder of Maja Blast, a genre-less party series built around trust, surprise, and dancing.
